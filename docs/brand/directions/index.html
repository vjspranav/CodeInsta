CodeInsta brand directions

Three ways to replace the generic code glyph on a blue tile. Each one starts from something systems people already use and trust: transit maps, engineering drawings and equipment labels. No purple, no sparkles, no code brackets. Every mark and wordmark below is drawn by hand in SVG; the app fonts are open-source and pinned.

Nothing in the app has changed. This page is a local preview for picking one (RUNBOOK D2).

Rating PlateThe app is a piece of equipment: every surface is labelled like a rating plate or an asset tag, with one caution-yellow plate as the brand.Caution yellow #FFC912

Recommendation: Interchange. It gives the app a structure, not just a logo. Categories become coloured lines and a series becomes stops in order, which is exactly the "part 2 never before part 1" rule the owner set. The mark survives at 32 px and as a monochrome themed icon. The green and white system has no purple, keeps the true-black OLED background the app already uses, and the solid pictograms make the nav and the reel rail read at a glance over video. Spool is the runner-up: the idea is charming, but its thin linework weakens at small sizes and the palette stays close to the current blue.

Why Binder copies your data only once

The kernel maps a buffer into the receiving process, so a transaction is copied once, from the sender straight into the receiver. Overpass 800 / 600 / 400 with Overpass Mono 400 for code.

err = writeTransactionData(BC_TRANSACTION, flags, handle, code, data, nullptr);

Overpass descends from Highway Gothic, the US road-sign alphabet, so it is built to be read at speed and at a distance. One family keeps the signage feel; the mono cut carries code.

SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Delve Withrington, Red Hat). Pinned as @fontsource/overpass@5.3.0 and @fontsource/overpass-mono@5.3.0; vendor the woff2/ttf files into web and Expo.

Icons

Custom solid pictogram set (10 icons). Transit pictograms are filled shapes read at a glance, and lucide is a line set at any stroke width. Thicker lucide strokes (2.5) only make it look heavy, not signed. The set is small and every shape here is drawn on a 24 grid with 2.5 strokes for the few open forms, with details cut out of the surface colour.

Screens

Trade-off. The series rule (part 2 never before part 1) and the category list already are a line map. The brand makes that structure visible.

Why Binder copies your data only once

err = writeTransactionData(BC_TRANSACTION, flags, handle, code, data, nullptr);

B612 was drawn for Airbus cockpit displays: open apertures, unambiguous glyphs, made for instruments rather than marketing. The wordmark follows the single-stroke capitals of engineering drawings.

SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Intactile Design for Airbus). Pinned as @fontsource/b612@5.3.0 and @fontsource/b612-mono@5.3.0; vendor the files.

Icons

lucide works: strokeWidth 1.5 with absoluteStrokeWidth, round caps, plus two small drawn details. Drawing linework is already thin and round-ended, which is what lucide is. Ship lucide-react at 1.5 so icons match the 1.5 px drawing lines at every size. Only the series progress (a dimension line) and the end card frame are custom; the samples here are drawn on lucide geometry at 1.5.

Why Binder copies your data only once

The kernel maps a buffer into the receiving process, so a transaction is copied once, from the sender straight into the receiver. Barlow Condensed 700 / 600 for display and labels, Barlow 400 / 500 for text, JetBrains Mono 400 for code.

err = writeTransactionData(BC_TRANSACTION, flags, handle, code, data, nullptr);

Barlow is a grotesk modelled on the plates and signs of California highways, so the condensed cut fits a stamped label. JetBrains Mono is already the owner's editor font.

SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Jeremy Tribby; JetBrains). Pinned as @fontsource/barlow-condensed@5.3.0, @fontsource/barlow@5.3.0 and @fontsource/jetbrains-mono@5.3.0; vendor the files.

Icons

Custom line set: lucide geometry where it fits, redrawn square where it does not. lucide with square caps and mitred joins at 2 px covers feed, inbox, share and sound. Its heart, compass, person and the round not-for-me sign are soft circles that fight the square capitals, so like, explore, profiles, ask, glossary and not-for-me are redrawn on the same 24 grid with square corners. Six custom paths, four from lucide.

Screens

Trade-off. The boldest and most legible at small sizes; the risk is that a yellow CI tile also reads as continuous integration, which this audience may enjoy or find confusing.

  • Android adaptive icons: 1024 px foreground and background layers (108 dp), with the key shape inside the 66 dp safe circle. Monochrome layer for themed icons. iOS: an opaque 1024 px master; the system applies the corner mask.
  • Contrast ratios are computed with the WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula. Body text needs 4.5:1; the like heart is a UI graphic and needs 3:1.
  • Creator avatars are the existing Claude and GPT marks from packages/shared/src/creator-marks.ts, unchanged.
  • Regenerate everything with node docs/brand/directions/build/generate.mjs from the repo root.
